Tips for Best Results

Good lighting

Make sure the marker is well-lit and avoid harsh shadows

Keep distance

Stay 20-50cm away from the marker for best tracking

Flat surface

Place markers on a flat, non-reflective surface

Steady hands

Hold your device steady for smoother playback
